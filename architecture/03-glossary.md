# 3. Glossary {#sec-glossary}

*Replace or extend. A glossary is a good last chapter for a document read by
people who do not live in the platform every day.*

Catalog
: The top level of Unity Catalog's three-part naming (`catalog.schema.table`).
  Each workspace in this platform is paired with one catalog.

DAB
: A Declarative Automation Bundle (formerly a Databricks Asset Bundle): a
  folder of YAML and code that describes jobs, pipelines and their settings,
  deployed with the Databricks CLI.

Environment
: One of dev, ppr or prd — its own subscription and network. They are
  described in @sec-environments.

Workspace
: One of sandbox, staging or live inside an environment. Three per
  environment.
